Questions

Before you book.

Do I need a gym?

No. A pair of dumbbells and a floor is enough to write you a program. If you have a full gym, even better — I'll use it.

I've never trained before. Is that a problem?

It's the easiest place to start. We begin with the movements you can do well today and add from there. The first month is about turning up three times a week, nothing more complicated than that.

Do I have to eat chicken and rice?

No. The targets matter, the specific food doesn't. We build the plan out of things you already eat, so you're not choking down meals you hate for six months.

What happens on the free call?

Thirty minutes. You tell me where you're at, what you've tried and what you want. I tell you what I'd do about it. No pressure to sign up at the end of it.

How do the check-ins work?

Once a week you send your weight, a couple of photos and a note on how training and food went. I come back with what changes for the week ahead — sets, calories, or nothing at all if it's working.
